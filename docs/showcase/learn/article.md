Adopting answer engine optimization does not mean abandoning traditional SEO. Instead, AEO builds directly on top of essential technical SEO foundations. Large language models rely on search indices and crawlable web structures to verify real-world facts.

## Preparing Your Brand for Conversational Discovery

Transitioning to answer engines requires three immediate architectural priorities:

### 1. Structure Machine-Readable Content

AI models prioritize semantically clear content. Implement nested JSON-LD schema across your product catalog, service offerings, and organizational profiles to remove ambiguity.

Adopt standardized resources like root llms.txt files. These Markdown-formatted directories provide clear summaries of your core documentation, making extraction simple for AI retrieval engines.

### 3. Audit Your AI Share of Voice Regularly

Do not guess how generative tools view your brand. Test buyer-intent prompts across leading models to identify where citations drop off.
