# Acceptable AI Use Policy — Marketing Content

**Version 1.0 — Approved [date] by [name, role] — Review annually.** Staff acknowledge this policy in the HR system; completion records are SOC 2 evidence (CC1.4, CC2.2).

1. **Approved tool only.** Client briefs, reference material and unreleased information may be used with AI only through the Marketing Content Writer Agent. Consumer AI tools and browser AI extensions are prohibited for client material.
2. **You own the output.** AI drafts are first drafts. The reviewer who approves a draft is accountable for its accuracy, originality and compliance with advertising rules. Every item in `claims_to_verify` must be checked before approval; the approval note must say how.
3. **Do not paste secrets.** API keys, credentials, card numbers and personal data of customers must not be entered. The gateway redacts what it can detect; detection is a backstop, not permission.
4. **Disclosure.** Published content retains the AI-assistance disclosure line unless the client contract specifies otherwise.
5. **Escalation.** If AI output looks wrong, biased, defamatory, or reveals information it should not have, do not publish; use the *Reject* action with a note and email security@[agency].
6. **Segregation of duties.** You may not approve or publish content you requested.
7. **Training.** Complete the "AI in marketing content" module before your account is granted the requester or reviewer role, and annually thereafter.

Violations are handled under the disciplinary policy.
